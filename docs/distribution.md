# Distribution plan — LaunchJury

We're reaching solo indie hackers and side-project developers who just finished building something and have never written a launch post before — people who live on Hacker News (lurking/commenting, rarely submitting), the Indie Hackers forum, Product Hunt, and dev Twitter/X. They are technically literate, allergic to marketing-speak, and — per the research — currently in an active, vocal backlash against AI-generated Show HN posts, so this exact audience has to be won over with peer-to-peer, evidence-backed, documentation-style writing rather than anything that reads like typical launch marketing.

## Channels
1. **Show HN** (Hacker News) — post the Show HN post on Tuesday at 07:00 AM America/Los_Angeles.
   - Why: This is the single highest-leverage channel for GitHub stars if it lands, but the category has a near-perfect record of dying here (CoLaunchly: 1pt/0 comments, PH tagline generators: 4pts/1 comment, pitch generators: 1-4pts for a decade). The only wins in this exact CLI-dev-tool category (DepsGuard 40pts, Runway 93pts) came from narrow scope, documentation-style titles, and founders answering every comment fast. HN is also in an active backlash against AI-generated posts, so this post must visibly demonstrate 'here's what LaunchJury generated vs. what I changed and why' rather than claim polish.
   - Expect: If positioned narrowly (lead with 'validates and scores your Show HN post against real comparable HN threads', not the 10-feature/7-persona list) and the account has real prior participation, expect a DepsGuard/Runway-shaped outcome: 20-90 points, skeptical-but-warm comments ('why would I pay for this', 'does this work offline', requests for self-hosted mode), and a chance to build goodwill by fixing a reported bug live in-thread. If it reads as 'another AI copy generator', expect a CoLaunchly-shaped outcome: 1 point, 0 comments, buried within the hour.
   - Tag/notify: No one to tag — HN has no notification mechanism and soliciting votes/comments from your network is against the rules, Do notify: nobody publicly; privately let 2-3 people who'll comment with genuine substance (not vote requests) know the post is live
   - Rules: No asking for upvotes, no vote brigading; One shot only — resubmitting a flopped or edited post gets flagged; Don't submit unless the tool fully works end-to-end — no landing-page-only submissions; Respond personally and fast to every comment, including bug reports, for the first several hours; Title and post should read like documentation (verb-first, exact tool names/commands), not a pitch
2. **Indie Hackers forum** (Indie Hackers) — post the LinkedIn post on Wednesday at 09:00 AM America/New_York.
   - Why: Research shows sustained, unmet demand here — the same 'what's on your launch checklist?' question has been re-asked across multiple threads over years — and self-promo framed as a build-in-public story or lesson-learned is explicitly tolerated. This is the safest place to soft-launch a few days before HN: it warms up early feedback, surfaces objections cheaply, and gives you material (real reactions) to cite honestly in the Show HN first comment. LaunchJury doesn't generate a dedicated IH asset, so adapt the narrative voice/structure of the drafted LinkedIn post into a first-person forum story rather than cross-posting it verbatim.
   - Expect: Modest but genuine engagement — a handful of comments sharing their own launch-checklist pain, some 'what stack did you use' questions, possibly someone volunteering to be an early tester. Low star impact directly, but useful signal and quotes to reuse.
   - Tag/notify: Indie Hackers 'Launch checklist' / 'Build in public' community threads
   - Rules: Frame as a story/lesson-learned, not an ad — self-promo is tolerated only in that framing; Don't drop a bare link; open with the problem you hit writing your own launch posts
3. **Product Hunt** (Product Hunt) — post the Product Hunt launch on Tuesday at 12:01 AM America/Los_Angeles.
   - Why: Second wave, one week after HN, so you can launch with an actual GitHub URL and (hopefully) some stars/social proof already in hand — PH voters and comments scrutinize maker responsiveness on launch day, and the community rewards taglines that sound like 'a builder describing a tool to other builders' over generic AI-style copy, which the drafted tagline must be edited to match.
   - Expect: Moderate upvotes if 10-20 supporters are lined up beforehand and the maker comment answers questions all day; low-effort/no-supporter launches in this exact adjacent category (PH tagline generators) got almost no traction, so the pre-launch supporter list matters more than the copy.
   - Tag/notify: Personal network of makers who've agreed in advance to check it out, Any Product Hunt hunter you have an existing relationship with — do not cold-solicit one
   - Rules: Never publicly ask for upvotes; Line up 10-20 supporters privately before launch, not by mass-DMing strangers; Maker comment must be active and substantive for the full 24h window
4. **X / Twitter thread** (X) — post the X / Twitter thread on Tuesday at 09:30 AM America/New_York.
   - Why: Dev Twitter overlaps heavily with the HN/PH audience and is the fastest way to route your own followers to the Show HN thread in its critical early-vote window; the growth-marketer jury persona in LaunchJury itself is built for exactly this asset. Post it the same morning as the HN submission so early HN traffic and X traffic compound instead of splitting attention across days.
   - Expect: A handful of retweets/replies from indie-dev following if the thread leads with a concrete, quotable claim (e.g. a real HN thread it referenced) rather than a feature list; the repo link in the final tweet should see a small but real click-through spike correlated with the HN post's front-page window.
   - Tag/notify: No specific handles to tag (avoid appearing to solicit retweets from larger accounts) — let it circulate organically among dev-tool/indie-hacker follows
   - Rules: Link goes in the last tweet, not the first; Reply to every comment for the first hour — this decides algorithmic reach
5. **Demo video (YouTube, linked from Show HN + PH)** (YouTube / embedded link) — post the Demo video script on Tuesday at 06:30 AM America/Los_Angeles.
   - Why: The research's clearest praise theme is that concrete, quantified, technical proof beats vague value-prop language (Runway's cost calculator, DepsGuard's specific tool list). A short terminal walkthrough showing a real `npx tsx src/cli.ts` run against a real repo — including an honest look at a jury round that failed and got revised — is the single best way to defuse the 'is this AI slop' objection HN will raise, because it shows the mechanism instead of describing it.
   - Expect: Not a traffic source on its own, but raises trust and dwell time for visitors arriving from HN/PH/X — expect it to be the thing top HN comments reference if the launch does land ('nice that you show the actual jury scoring').
   
   - Rules: No music-video-style editing or hype voiceover — screen-record the real CLI output, technical-plain tone matching the product's own stated tone
6. **LinkedIn** (LinkedIn) — post the LinkedIn post on Tuesday at 08:00 AM America/New_York.
   - Why: Lowest direct star-driver of the set, but low-cost incremental reach into a professional network that may include engineering managers/devs who star and share tools; native text posts with the link in the first comment outperform link-posts here per the general heuristic.
   - Expect: A few dozen reactions/comments from your existing professional network, occasional 'saving this for my own launch' replies; unlikely to move the star count much on its own but compounds with the other channels on launch day.
   
   - Rules: Native text post, link in first comment not the post body

## Sequence
- Day -14: **Hacker News** — Start commenting genuinely (not about LaunchJury) on other Show HN threads and relevant discussions to build real, non-self karma before the launch account is scrutinized. (Directly addresses the 'account risk, not product risk' finding — low-karma submitters get buried regardless of post quality.)
- Day -7: **LaunchJury (internal)** — Run LaunchJury on its own repo end-to-end; keep every jury round's JSON artifact so the founder can honestly show a 'failed then revised' example in the eventual first comment. (Produces the concrete 'here's what it generated, here's what I changed, why' material needed to defuse the recursion-trap objection.)
- Day -6: **Indie Hackers forum** — Publish a build-in-public retrospective story on Indie Hackers about building LaunchJury and the launch-checklist problem it solves. (Soft launch; framed as story/lesson-learned per IH norms, not an ad.)
- Day -4: **Demo video / YouTube** — Record and edit the terminal-walkthrough demo video, including one real jury-fail-then-revise cycle. (Concrete, quantified proof beats vague value-prop language per Runway/DepsGuard praise themes.)
- Day -3: **Show HN / Product Hunt prep** — Finalize the Show HN title/post/first comment using the recommended narrow positioning; line up 10-20 private Product Hunt supporters for the following week's launch. (Lead with the narrow claim (scores your post against real comparable threads), not the full feature list.)
- Day -2: **Private network / Discord** — Quietly share the repo with a few trusted indie-dev friends/Discords for a final sanity check and organic early stars. (Not vote-brigading — real feedback from people who'll engage substantively if the HN thread happens.)
- Day -1: **GitHub repo** — Final QA of README, LICENSE, working `npx tsx src/cli.ts` install path, and demo video link. (HN explicitly punishes non-working submissions.)
- Day +0: **Hacker News** — Submit Show HN at 07:00 AM PT; respond personally to every comment for the first several hours. (Tue morning is the slowest front-page turnover window per heuristics.)
- Day +0: **X / Twitter** — Publish the X thread ~09:30 AM ET once the HN post has initial traction; reply to all replies for an hour.
- Day +0: **LinkedIn** — Cross-post the LinkedIn native version, link in first comment.
- Day +1: **Hacker News** — Continue answering any late HN comments; ship a visible fix if any bug was reported and comment back on the thread. (Mirrors the DepsGuard pattern of live bug-fix goodwill.)
- Day +2: **Indie Hackers forum** — Post an Indie Hackers recap: what happened during the HN launch, real numbers, what you'd change.
- Day +6: **Product Hunt** — Launch on Product Hunt at 12:01 AM PT with supporters notified privately; maker comment active all day. (One week after HN so the launch has real stars/social proof by then.)
- Day +7: **Dev newsletters / Discords** — Reach out to relevant dev-tool newsletters/Discords with the launch story and real numbers now that there's traction to pitch. (Second-wave curators respond better to a proven story than a cold pitch.)

## Do not
- Don't post to r/SideProject or the r/IndieHackers subreddit as a link/ad on launch day — this run's Reddit research returned HTTP 403 on every query, so there is no confirmed evidence these subs currently tolerate project posts, and the broader pattern in this category (generic AI copy/marketing generators) is near-zero engagement or removal. Revisit only after HN traction exists, and only as a lesson-learned/discussion post, never a link drop.
- Don't post to general-purpose subs like r/programming or r/webdev — no positive evidence of tolerance was found and self-promotional project posts are routinely removed there.
- Don't submit the Show HN post from a new or low-non-self-karma account — HN readers explicitly check submitter history and dismiss low-karma self-promoters regardless of post quality; build genuine comment history for at least 1-2 weeks beforehand.
- Don't resubmit or repost the Show HN thread if it underperforms — resubmissions get flagged, and this is explicitly a one-shot channel.
- Don't ask for upvotes, votes, or 'support' anywhere (HN, PH, X, LinkedIn) — against HN/PH rules and reads as manipulation to this audience.
- Don't lead the Show HN title or opening paragraph with the 10-feature/7-persona breadth — this reads as 'LLM-wrapper' scope-as-weakness to an audience that rewards narrow tools (DepsGuard); lead with the single concrete claim (validates your launch post against real comparable HN/PH threads) and mention the rest as 'also does X.'
- Don't let any drafted asset go out reading as obviously AI-polished marketing copy — HN is in an active, vocal backlash against AI-generated Show HN posts; every asset needs a visible human edit pass and an explicit 'here's what the tool generated, here's what I changed, here's why' note.
- Don't launch on Product Hunt and Hacker News on the same day — splits the one shot HN gives you and PH supporters' attention; sequence them a week apart as planned.
